# Paper Fleet — taglines & landing copy

Working doc for headlines, subheads, and brand lines. Pair a **short, ownable
headline** with a **flowing subhead** that explains and sells.

_Last updated: 2026-10-24_

---

## Headline candidates (short, punchy — billboard-ready)

- **Command your fleet.** ← most ownable (product name as a verb); current lean
- **From backtest to battle.** — the whole pipeline in four words
- **Copy the winners. Skip the guesswork.** — the copy/social hook
- **Build trading bots. Train them. Watch them win.** — current live headline

Others in reserve: _Build. Train. Battle._ · _Backtest to battle._ · _Where strategies come to win._ · _Trade like a pro, without being one._

## Subheads (flowing / premium — full sentences with voice)

- Build a bot, prove it can trade, and deploy only the ones that earn it.
- Every strategy earns its place on the board — or it never trades a dollar.
- Turn a trading idea into a bot with a track record to back it.
- Design a strategy, let the market be the judge, and keep what wins.
- Copy a winning strategy, make it your own, and send it into battle.
- A fleet of trading bots, each one proven before it ever fights for real.
- Good strategies leave receipts — build one that does.
- Where a trading idea grows up into a bot that earns its keep.
- The conviction of a quant desk, without the desk.
- Trade like a room full of pros, run by one person: you.

## Notes / voice

- Concrete and benefit-first. Aspirational ("trade like a pro", "command your
  fleet") beats earnest virtue claims.
- **Avoid** self-congratulatory honesty lines. "Honest by design" was removed
  from the footer — announcing honesty reads as the opposite. Let the receipts
  (verified track records) do that work.
- Footer stamp currently: _"Paper money only. Not investment advice."_ (plain
  disclaimer, placeholder until a real stamp is chosen).
- No profit guarantees / "get rich" language — keep it about building, proving,
  and competing, not promised returns.
