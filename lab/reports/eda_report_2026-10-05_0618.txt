==============================================================================
  15. MIXTURE EVIDENCE — this dataset is several behaviors, not one
==============================================================================
Any pooled mean/median above describes a BLEND. The cuts below show the
distinct ingredients. Proper separation (path clustering) is Phase 3 work.

--- Timing: where the immediate move happens (direction-adjusted means) ---
  candle open -> fill (before entry):   -828.178%  (hit 36%)
  fill -> first 15m close (after):      +1.295%  (hit 62%)
  30m -> 24h adds (median path):        +0.19% -> +0.41%
  -> typical entry comes AFTER an adverse micro-move; bounce is immediate,
     then a slow drift continues for hours. Two different clocks.

--- Ingredients by entry type (pre-entry micro-move vs trade direction) ---
  segment                           n  30m med  30m mean  24h med  24h mean
  FADE (into adverse move)      4,906    +0.63     +1.91    +0.49     +1.06
  FLAT                          6,151    +0.06     +0.12    +0.35     +0.43
  CHASE (with move)             1,689    +0.04     -1.94    +0.57     -0.97
  FADE = fast bounce edge (execution-sensitive, fat-tailed).
  FLAT = slow drift edge (latency-tolerant, most shippable).
  CHASE = negative-mean failure mode (ban it).
  Mean/median gaps inside every segment = still mixtures; cluster in Phase 3.
